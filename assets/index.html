    Parts are contiguous by default: each begins where the previous one ends, so nothing is dropped between editors. Any remainder from an uneven division is spread over the first parts, so the final time always lands on your episode end. "Copy for email" puts the styled table on your clipboard (same colours and layout as the task emails), so pasting it into Gmail brings the design with it. Switch an editor off to drop them from the week without deleting them, and drag the handle (or use the arrow keys on it) to change who takes which part.
