EPISODE PART SPLITTER - MAC

How to start
  1. Unzip, then drag "Episode Part Splitter" into your Applications folder (or leave it anywhere).
  2. Double-click it.

First time only
  macOS may say it "cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified",
  because the app is not notarized with a paid Apple account. Do this once:
    - Right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose Open, then click Open.
  If it says the app is "damaged", open Terminal and run this once, then open it again:
    xattr -cr "/Applications/Episode Part Splitter.app"

Needs
  macOS 14.5 (Sonoma) or newer. Works on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

It looks and works exactly like the web page:
  - "Copy for email" copies the styled table; paste it into Gmail or Mail.
  - Your team, times and options are remembered between launches.
  - Settings are kept in:  ~/Library/Application Support/EpisodeSplitter   (delete that folder to reset)

To remove the app, drag it to the Trash.
